A Long-Term Investment in Integrated Development

Senyu Health Town brings together agriculture, industry, education, cultural tourism, and medical and wellness in a single integrated site in Yiwu, Zhejiang, China. It stands as evidence of Senyu Holding Group's long-term resources, broad industry experience, and readiness to grow with international partners.

Where Tradition Meets Long-Term Development

Senyu Health Town connects traditional Chinese medicine with scientific research, production, and culture in one long-term development. It reflects the same foundation that supports Senyu Holding Group's 30 years of experience in traditional Chinese medicine and the health and wellness industry.

For international partners, Senyu Health Town demonstrates Senyu Holding Group's capacity to invest at scale and to sustain integrated industry development over the long term.

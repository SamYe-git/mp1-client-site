# Client Brief

**Client:** Senyu Holding Group is a Chinese company with 30 years of experience in traditional Chinese medicine and the health and wellness industry. Its health product brand is Senshan.

**Purpose:** Create an English-language corporate introduction website that presents Senyu Holding Group to potential international business partners and builds credibility for future global-market expansion.

**Audience:** The primary audience is potential international business partners. Secondary audiences are distributors and consumers.

**Key action:** A visitor should email Senyu Holding Group to begin a partnership conversation.

## Company Strengths

The website should communicate that Senyu Holding Group has:

- 30 years of experience in traditional Chinese medicine and health and wellness
- Advanced technology
- A scientific foundation for its products
- Strong product quality standards
- Large-scale factory production capacity
- Proven success in the Chinese market
- A role in developing standards for the Chinese Dendrobium officinale industry
- Long-term investment in health, agriculture, tourism, and related industries

## Pages Needed

### Home

The Home page will introduce Senyu Holding Group and explain its 30-year background in the health and wellness industry.

It will include:

- A clear introduction to Senyu Holding Group
- Key trust signals about technology, science, quality, scale, and market presence
- Links to the Health Products and Senyu Health Town pages
- A visible call to action for partnership inquiries

### Health Products

The Health Products page will introduce the Senshan product brand.

It will focus on three product categories:

- Granules
- Capsules
- Beverages

The page will use approved product images and factual information from the existing company website. It will also explain the company’s research, quality, and production background.

### Senyu Health Town

The Senyu Health Town page will present the Health Town as evidence of the company’s scale and long-term vision.

It will include:

- Approximately 4.06 square kilometers of total area
- Approximately RMB 5 billion in investment
- Medical care
- Education
- Real estate
- Agriculture
- Tourism
- Antioxidant-related industries

This page will show international business partners that Senyu Holding Group has long-term resources, broad industry experience, and strong development capacity.

### Contact / Partnership

The Contact / Partnership page will invite potential international partners to contact the company.

It will include:

- A short invitation for business partnership inquiries
- The official partnership email address when it is confirmed
- Optional phone number, address, and contact person information

## Content Status

- Existing group branding and color direction
- Company, product, and Health Town images to be approved by the client

### Content Still Needed

## Style Preferences

The website should feel:

- Natural
- Premium
- Technology-driven

The design should use Senyu Holding Group’s existing brand colors and approved images. It should balance the natural origin of traditional Chinese medicine with the professionalism, scale, and technology expected by international business partners.

## Inspiration Sites

The client did not select individual reference websites during the interview. The design direction approved by the client is a clean, modern, international corporate website with:

- Large, high-quality photography
- Short and evidence-based text
- Clear sections for company strengths, products, and the Health Town
- A premium visual system based on existing group brand colors
- Clear contact calls to action for potential partners

Before final submission, this section will be updated with three to five specific inspiration websites and the design elements the client approves.

# Layout Plan

## Layout Sketch

A hand-drawn layout sketch will be created and committed to the repository as `layout-sketch.jpg` before any webpage code is generated.

## Home Page Layout

The Home page will begin with a large hero image showing a Senshan product, Dendrobium officinale, or the Senyu Health Town.

The hero section will include:

- A short headline introducing Senyu Holding Group
- A statement about 30 years of experience in health and wellness
- A call to action inviting potential partners to contact the company

Below the hero section, the page will include:

1. Key company facts and trust signals
2. A section linking to Health Products
3. A section linking to Senyu Health Town
4. A short section about technology, research, and quality
5. A partnership contact call to action
6. A footer with navigation and company information

## Health Products Page Layout

The Health Products page will introduce the Senshan brand and use real product images.

The page will include:

1. A page title and introduction
2. Product cards for granules, capsules, and beverages
3. A section about research, quality, and production
4. A partnership contact call to action
5. A shared footer

## Senyu Health Town Page Layout

The Senyu Health Town page will begin with a large landscape image and a short statement about its approximately 4.06-square-kilometer scale and approximately RMB 5 billion investment.

The page will include:

1. A page title and introduction
2. Key facts about the Health Town
3. A grid showing medical care, education, real estate, agriculture, tourism, and antioxidant-related industries
4. A section explaining the project’s value as corporate credibility
5. A partnership contact call to action
6. A shared footer

## Contact / Partnership Page Layout

The Contact / Partnership page will be simple and professional.

The page will include:

1. A heading for international partnership inquiries
2. A short invitation for potential partners to contact the company
3. The official contact email when it is confirmed
4. Optional phone number and address information
5. A shared footer

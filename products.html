Quality Rooted in Research

Senshan products are developed on a scientific foundation and produced under strong quality standards, backed by Senyu Holding Group's large-scale factory production capacity.

Senyu Holding Group has played a role in developing standards for the Chinese Dendrobium officinale industry, and Dendrobium officinale is a core natural-origin ingredient across the Senshan product line.

Our Product Categories

Granules

Made from Dendrobium officinale (Tie Pi Feng Dou), Senshan granules are packaged in individual sachets and gift-ready boxes for convenient daily use.

Capsules

Senshan capsules concentrate the same Dendrobium officinale foundation into individually sealed capsule sachets, designed for everyday convenience.

Beverages

Guocao, Senshan's ready-to-drink line, bottles a Dendrobium officinale, wolfberry, and white peony beverage in a portable 310mL format.

## AI decision for #1: Interactive Health Town map

Options were: (a) clickable icons over the masterplan image, (b) an HTML `<map>/<area>` image map, (c) traced SVG shapes, and (d) Google Maps/Leaflet. I chose (a) because it uses only HTML/CSS/JS and works on phones. I stopped before placing the icons because the client has not confirmed which area is the school, hospital, mall, or real estate; the feature is listed in “Later” in `PRD.md`.

Introducing Senyu Holding Group

For 30 years, Senyu Holding Group has built its foundation in traditional Chinese medicine and the health and wellness industry. The group combines advanced technology with a scientific foundation for its products, supported by strong quality standards and large-scale factory production capacity.

Senyu Holding Group has proven success in the Chinese market and has played a role in developing standards for the Chinese Dendrobium officinale industry — reflecting a long-term commitment to health, agriculture, tourism, and related industries.
